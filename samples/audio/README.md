# Audio classification samples

These clips are small fixtures for manually testing the configured Apple
SoundAnalysis labels. They are not a representative accuracy benchmark.

## ESC-50 clips

The first three files come from the official
[ESC-50 repository](https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50). ESC-50 distributes
the dataset under
[CC BY-NC 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/); its
[LICENSE](https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50/blob/master/LICENSE) records the
underlying source and attribution for each clip.

| Local file | ESC-50 file | Category | Underlying source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cat_meow.wav` | `2-110010-A-5.wav` | cat | "cat meow II" by tuberatanka, CC0 |
| `dog_bark.wav` | `1-100032-A-0.wav` | dog | "rose_bark.wav" by nfrae, CC0 |
| `engine_accelerating.wav` | `3-141240-A-44.wav` | engine | "Renault 19 underhood acceleration" by lovretta, CC0 |

## Formula One clip

`race_car_ferrari_f60.wav` is the first 12 seconds of
[Ferrari F60 (2009)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_F60_(2009).ogg),
recorded by Edvvc (Ed Pond). The original is available under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). This derivative
was trimmed, converted from Ogg Vorbis to PCM WAV, downmixed to mono, and
resampled to 16 kHz. It remains available under CC BY-SA 3.0.
